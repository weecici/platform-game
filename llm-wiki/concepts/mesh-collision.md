# Exact Mesh Collision

Visual 3D models (GLTF, OBJ, etc.) loaded via the `ModelLoader` do not inherently have physical presence in the Cannon.js physics world. To make them solid obstacles that entities like the player can collide with, we parse their geometric data into exact physics representations.

## Mechanism

When a `DecorationDef` of `type: 'model'` has `solid: true`, the `LevelManager` automatically generates a `CANNON.Trimesh` for every `THREE.Mesh` found within the loaded model.

1. **Wait for Loading:** The model is fetched asynchronously and added to the scene anchor.
2. **Apply World Transforms:** `model.updateMatrixWorld(true)` is called so the local vertices of the child meshes map perfectly to their final scene positions (taking into account the model's scale, position, and rotation relative to its anchor).
3. **Extract Geometry:** For each mesh, the raw `position` array and `index` array are iterated over. The vertices are multiplied by the `matrixWorld` to convert them to world-space coordinates.
4. **Trimesh Creation:** A `CANNON.Trimesh(vertices, indices)` is created from this transformed geometry and attached to a static `CANNON.Body` (`mass: 0`). This static body remains at the physics world origin (0,0,0) because the trimesh vertices are already offset to their correct world coordinates.

## Limitations & Compatibility

- **Trimesh Collisions:** In Cannon.js, `CANNON.Trimesh` colliders are extremely precise but only support collisions against `Sphere` and `Plane` objects. They *do not* collide with other `Trimesh`, `Box`, or `Cylinder` shapes out of the box. 
- Fortunately, our `PlayerController` utilizes a `CANNON.Sphere` for its physical presence, allowing the player to perfectly interact with any mesh collision generated by this system.
- **Performance:** Complex scene models with tens of thousands of vertices can slow down the physics broadphase. Large solid models should ideally be low-poly proxies if performance becomes an issue.
